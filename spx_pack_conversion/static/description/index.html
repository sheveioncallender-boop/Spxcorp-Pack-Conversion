Spxcorp Pack Conversion

Separate stock. Independent prices. Open packs when you need loose items.

Built for Odoo 19 Community by Spxcorp Limited.

Cases, boxes, strips and singles

Link existing products or create a pack from an item. Purchase and receive cases normally, or start from opening stock without a purchase order. Convert through a simple form backed by native Odoo stock and manufacturing operations.

  • Independent barcodes, selling prices and stock balances.
  • Whole-pack conversion and optional repacking.
  • Available-stock and reservation checks.
  • Batch lineage and expiration-date carryover.
  • Cost-value checks and atomic posting.
  • Operator history and manager-controlled reversals.
